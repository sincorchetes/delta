Your backups are **encrypted**. Delta keeps their passphrase in your desktop keyring and uses it on its own,
so you never type it.

## Why you need the recovery key

If you lose access to your keyring (a new computer, a reinstall, a damaged profile), the passphrase goes with it.
The **recovery key** that the assistant gave you is then **the only way to read your backups**.

<Aside type="danger" title="Store it away from this computer">
A recovery key kept only on the disk that fails, or on the computer you lose, protects nothing.
Print it, or keep it in a password manager or on a USB drive somewhere else.
</Aside>

## Use your backups after a reinstall

1. Install Delta and open it.
2. In the assistant, choose the folder or drive that holds your backups.
3. Delta finds the **existing repository**: enter the recovery key (or your passphrase) as **Repository passphrase**
   and choose **Use this repository**. Nothing in it is erased.

## What Delta never does

- It never stores the passphrase in a plain file, in the settings or in a command line.
- It never sends your backups anywhere: they stay in the folder or drive you chose.
- It never overwrites your files when you restore.
