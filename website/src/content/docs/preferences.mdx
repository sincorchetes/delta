## Destination

**Backups are kept in** shows where your copies live. It reads **Not set up yet** until you finish the assistant.

## Schedule

In **Back up automatically**, choose how often: **Every hour**, **Every day**, **Every week** or **Custom**.
A custom schedule is a systemd `OnCalendar` expression. If one was set outside Delta, Preferences says so and
keeps it until you choose one of the options.

## Keep old backups

Older backups are removed to save space. Pick how much history to keep:

| Option | History kept |
|---|---|
| **Short** | Every hour for half a day and every day for a week. |
| **Standard** | Every hour for a day, every day for a week, every week for a month and every month for a year. |
| **Long** | Every hour for a day, every day for two weeks, every week for three months and every month for three years. |

## Folders to back up

Add folders with **Add folder…** to copy only those. **Leave the list empty to back up your whole personal folder.**

## Skip these

Add folders (**Add folder…**) or single files (**Add file…**) that are not backed up, nor is anything inside them. **The cache and the trash are always skipped.**
You cannot skip everything you back up: Delta refuses with *That would skip everything you back up.*
