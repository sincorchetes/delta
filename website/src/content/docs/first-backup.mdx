The first time you open Delta it says **Backups are not set up**. Choose **Set up backups** and the
assistant walks you through four short pages with **Next** and **Back**. Nothing is created until you choose **Create backups** on the last one.

<Steps>

1. **Where should backups go?**

   Pick a folder or a drive on this computer.

   - **Folder**: choose **Choose…** and pick a **new or empty** folder.
   - **Connected drives**: removable drives appear here. Backups go in a folder called `delta-backups` on the drive you pick.

   <Shot name="wizard-destination" alt="The first page of the assistant, “Where should backups go?”, with a Folder row and a list of connected drives." />

   Delta tells you right away if the path is not a full path, does not exist, is a file, is a whole disk, or is a folder that is not empty.

2. **What should be backed up?**

   By default, **Everything in your home folder**. To choose, add folders with **Choose a folder to back up**.
   Add the folders you care about and leave the rest out.

3. **Your recovery key**

   Delta protects your backups with a key kept in your keyring. If you ever lose access to it, this
   recovery key is the only way to read them.

   <Shot name="wizard-recovery" alt="The assistant page “Your recovery key”, with the key, the Copy and “Save to a file…” buttons and the confirmation switch." />

   Use **Save to a file…**, then store the file somewhere safe, **away from this computer**. You cannot finish until you
   choose **I have saved my recovery key**, or accept the risk with **Or I accept that I could lose access to my backups without it.**
   **Copy** alone does not count as saving it.

   Prefer your own passphrase? Choose **Use my own passphrase instead**, type it twice (it has a minimum length) and do not lose it.

4. **Ready to go**

   Delta creates the destination and starts protecting your files. **Setting up your backups…** can take a moment.

</Steps>

## Using a backup you already have

If the folder you picked already holds a Delta backup (after reinstalling, for example), the assistant offers
**Use the existing repository**. Enter its passphrase and choose **Use this repository**. **Nothing in it is erased.**

<Aside type="tip">
The first backup runs on the schedule you chose, or right away with **Back up now**. Afterwards,
[the status window](../status/) tells you when the last and the next one happen.
</Aside>
